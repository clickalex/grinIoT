# Chosen Direction: Signal Garden

## Design Movement

Signal Garden combines **biophilic minimalism** with **editorial systems design**. It borrows the discipline of a technical operating console, but avoids cyberpunk tropes in favor of tactile, quiet technology and weathered natural materials.

## Core Principles

1. **The story moves like a field operation.** The page follows a deliberate sequence from problem, to system, to rollout, to investor logic, to source documents.
2. **Technology is legible, never theatrical.** Data, rules, and controls appear as precise instrument panels, with restrained emphasis and clear hierarchy.
3. **Nature supplies the contrast.** Large high-fidelity garden imagery interrupts analytical passages, reminding visitors that the system protects living spaces.
4. **Evidence earns attention.** Metrics, milestones, and operational guardrails use structured blocks instead of decorative claims.

## Color Philosophy

The visual field is **near-black spruce** to represent dependable outdoor hardware and nighttime monitoring. A single **Signal Lime** accent expresses plant vitality and active device status, while **Sunlit Amber** distinguishes investment, milestones, and intervention. Warm ivory makes long-form internal and investor documents comfortable to read without turning the interface into a bright generic dashboard.

## Layout Paradigm

The site is a **vertical signal trail**, not a centered marketing landing page. A thin illuminated route advances down the left edge on desktop, serving as a persistent chapter index. Each chapter alternates between off-axis narrative columns, full-bleed image frames, and stacked evidence cards. At smaller widths, the route becomes a compact top progress marker.

## Signature Elements

1. **Signal Trail:** A green-to-amber vertical line with numbered chapters and faint measurement ticks.
2. **Telemetry Capsules:** Thin, squared information panels with a small status light and all-caps label.
3. **Root Grid:** A subtle lattice of short horizontal and vertical lines behind key content, inspired by irrigation diagrams and garden plans.

## Interaction Philosophy

Navigation is always obvious and lightweight. Chapter jumps smooth-scroll to the relevant sequence. The documents section uses a clear switching system that lets users move between the Internal Document, Investor Brief, and Roadmap without leaving the page. Each content panel prioritizes reading and download access over novelty.

## Animation

The signal trail quietly brightens as the user moves through the narrative. Cards enter with a short upward fade and a 40–70ms stagger; images reveal through a horizontal mask rather than scaling. Buttons depress slightly on activation. All nonessential movement disables for visitors who prefer reduced motion. No animation should exceed 320ms.

## Typography System

**Manrope** is used for compact interface labels, navigation, and data because it remains clear at small sizes. **DM Sans** handles explanatory copy and document body text. **Fraunces** provides an organic display contrast for major statements and investor-facing section titles. Headlines use sentence case with deliberate line breaks; labels use small uppercase tracking.

## Brand Essence

**Grinrex IoT is the modular, water-intelligent operating system for people growing more with less space and less waste.**

**Personality:** measured, regenerative, quietly capable.

## Brand Voice

Headlines should sound like operational truths rather than promotional slogans. CTAs should invite the next informed step, and microcopy should explain what a reader will see or control.

> “Every drop has a destination.”

> “Follow the system from garden signal to measurable outcome.”

Generic filler such as “Welcome to our website” and “Get started today” is not used.

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a bold, text-free **sprouting sensor loop**: a single geometric loop that reads simultaneously as a leaf, a water droplet, and a connected circuit. The wordmark uses a custom letter-spaced Manrope treatment in the interface, while the standalone symbol appears large in the header and hero.

## Signature Brand Color

**Signal Lime — #B8F15A**. It is reserved for active readings, the route line, and high-priority calls to action.

## Content Sequence

1. Opening thesis and navigation.
2. Urban-growing problem and Grinrex solution.
3. Modular operating system and data flow.
4. Value modules and product packaging.
5. Execution roadmap.
6. Investor narrative: commercial model, investment logic, and validation milestones.
7. Internal project document: MVP, operating principles, risks, and mitigation.
8. Document library: internal brief, investor brief, and roadmap, presented in readable panels with download controls.
9. Closing call to action.
